Work–Life Tipping Point

Two feedback loops decide whether a workload is sustainable. The diagram shows the loops. The model below shows where the balance tips.

Causal loop diagram

+ means the two variables move in the same direction. − means they move in opposite directions. The thicker loop is the one in control at the current slider settings.

B: Work harder (balancing)

More work outstanding leads to more hours worked. More hours complete more work, and the outstanding work falls. This loop keeps the workload under control.

R: Burnout (reinforcing)

More hours worked leave less personal time. Energy falls, then productivity. Less work is completed, so outstanding work grows and hours rise again. This loop feeds itself.

Find your tipping point

Move the two sliders. The balance holds while the balancing loop can absorb the workload. It tips when the reinforcing loop takes over.
